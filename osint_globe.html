LEGAL OBLIGATIONS

FARA data from EDGAR and sanctions data from OpenSanctions carry no restrictions. Tallinn Rules 32–33 govern state-level attribution assertions — OSINT findings must be distinguished from policy-level attribution decisions. ANALYTICAL REFERENCES
SCORING FRAMEWORK
Domain scores are assessed against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF 2.0) Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover functions. Maturity tiers align with CISA's Election Security Risk Management Framework and the Center for Internet Security (CIS) Controls v8.
THREAT CORRELATION METHODOLOGY
Threat pressure scores are derived from real-time signal weighting across the eight OSINT strategies mapped to each domain. Signal confidence tiers (HIGH ×3, MED ×2, LOW ×1) are weighted per the ODNI Intelligence Community Directive 203 analytic standards. Gap Priority = threat pressure × readiness gap, following the risk matrix approach in ISO/IEC 27005:2022.
LEGAL & DOCTRINAL STANDARDS
Attribution confidence levels follow Tallinn Manual 3.0 Rules 30–37 and 84 (NATO CCDCOE, 2023). Disinformation domain scoring draws on the EU EEAS FIMI Threat Landscape Report 2023 and the European Democracy Action Plan indicator set. Foreign interference typologies reference Stanford Internet Observatory IO taxonomies.
DOMAIN DEFINITIONS
Network Security, Access Control, and Audit & Recovery domains are scoped to election management systems as defined in CISA's Election Infrastructure Security Guide (2024). Incident Response benchmarks align with NIST SP 800-61r3. Threat Intelligence operationalisation SLAs draw on EI-ISAC membership requirements. Supply Chain Risk indicators are mapped to NIST SP 800-161r1 (Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management).
DOCUMENT EXTRACTION MODEL
AI-assisted score extraction uses Anthropic Claude Sonnet 4 via the Messages API. The extraction prompt is grounded in the NIST CSF maturity scale and applies structured zero-shot scoring. Confidence ratings (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW) reflect evidence density in the source document; LOW confidence indicates inference from indirect indicators only. Analysts should validate extracted scores against the source document before operational use.
